Artist Statement

I am a Bangladeshi student and photographer working primarily in street and documentary photography. My practice focuses on everyday life, public spaces, labor, movement, and small human interactions within changing social environments. Through observation and atmosphere, I aim to create images that balance documentary realism with a personal and emotional sense of place.
